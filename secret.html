A Letter For You

(yes, this is really for you)

Hi. I’ve been trying to say this for a while, so here it goes…

We always chat — but we never really talk, like, personal. And honestly? I’ve really wanted to. So here I am.

I like you. A lot. More than a friend, more than a maybe. Every time we talk, my whole day gets a little brighter, and I keep catching myself smiling for no reason — and then realizing the reason is you. 🥰

Also… you’re so cute, and those big cheeks of yours? I keep thinking about how badly I want to just squish them. 🙈

I’d love to be something more with you. Holding your hand, lazy talks, silly jokes, all of it — I want that with you.

So… will you be mine? 🌸
forever yours,
me 💖
